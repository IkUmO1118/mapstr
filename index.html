what's this?

This is an app that allows you to record your daily life and travel memories and view them on a map.

how does it work?

  • First, click on the map(where you want to save the memory).
  • Then the form will appear, select the type of memory you want to save(daily of travel) and fill ti out.
  • (You may upload up to 30 seconds of short video and 5 photos in one submission.)
  • Once you have written your information and uploaded your photos and videos,press enter to display your memory in the list.
  • Once you have entered the memory, a menu will appear.
  • Once you tap on a thumbnail photo, you will see detailed information about the photo, video, and diary or memo you uploaded.
  • You can change the photo, dialy, etc.from the page with detailed information
